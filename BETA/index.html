WindyBox 3D Physical Operating Model

1. Static Globe Mechanics: The Earth planet world is stationary, allowing precise observation and manual interaction across latitude and longitude zones.

2. Sea Surface Temperature (SST): Tropical cyclones intensify over warm water (>26.5°C) and rapidly decay when crossing terrain or cold water.

3. Cyclone Steering & Beta Drift: Storm centers move under steering winds combined with poleward & westward Beta Drift generated by Coriolis gradients.

4. Atmospheric Pressure Layers: Switch between surface (1000 hPa), mid-troposphere steering (500 hPa), and upper jet streams (300 hPa) to inspect vector fields.
