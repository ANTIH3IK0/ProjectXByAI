Synoptic Atmospheric Principles

WindyBox 3D Physical Dynamics Manual

1. Sea Surface Temperature & Land Elevation Engine: Tropical systems drastically intensify over warm water (SST > 26.5°C). Hitting land or cold water causes systems to decay and their radius to expand.

2. System Types: Subtropical systems act as hybrids over warm water. Extratropical (Frontal) cyclones grow vastly in size and intensify from mid-latitude temperature gradients.

3. Vortex Steering & Beta Drift: Tropical cyclones move along steering currents plus Beta Drift (poleward and westward drift from Coriolis gradients).

4. ENSO Oscillations: El Niño warms the central Pacific and weakens trade winds; La Niña cools it and strengthens tropical easterlies.
