Synoptic Atmospheric Principles

WindyBox 3D Physical Dynamics Manual

1. Global Circulation Cells: Visualizes surface trade winds (Hadley Cell), mid-latitude westerlies, and polar easterlies with Ekman surface boundary deflection (~22.5° angle).

2. Atmospheric Pressure Levels: Toggle between 1000 hPa (surface), 500 hPa (mid-troposphere steering), and 300 hPa (upper jet stream) to analyze vertical wind shear.

3. Vortex Steering & Beta Drift: Tropical cyclones move along environmental steering currents plus Beta Drift (poleward and westward drift from the latitudinal Coriolis gradient).

4. ENSO Oscillations: El Niño weakens Pacific trade winds while La Niña strengthens tropical easterlies and monsoon flow.
