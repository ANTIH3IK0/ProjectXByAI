Synoptic Atmospheric Principles

WindyBox 3D Physical Dynamics Manual

1. Sea Surface Temperature & Land Elevation Engine: Tropical systems will drastically intensify and shrink over extremely warm water (SST > 26.5°C). Conversely, hitting land (high elevation) or cold water will cause systems to decay (pressure rises) and their radius to rapidly expand as they lose structure.

2. System Types: Subtropical systems act as hybrids over marginally warm waters. Extratropical (Frontal) cyclones ignore SST and intensify mainly from baroclinic (temperature gradient) forcing in mid-to-high latitudes, growing vastly in size.

3. Vortex Steering & Beta Drift: Tropical cyclones move along environmental steering currents plus Beta Drift (poleward and westward drift from the latitudinal Coriolis gradient).

4. ENSO Oscillations: El Niño warms the central Pacific and weakens trade winds, while La Niña cools it and strengthens tropical easterlies.
